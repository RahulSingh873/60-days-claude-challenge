📋 Quarterly Growth Strategy Meeting

Meeting transcript analysis — structured action plan and decisions

📅 Not specified 👥 7 Speakers 🎯 Q2 Review + Q3 Planning
📝 Summary

The leadership team reviewed Q2 performance, which saw 12% revenue growth against an 18% target. The shortfall was driven by 5 enterprise deals slipping into Q3 due to procurement delays and additional security reviews. Website lead generation improved (+34% traffic) but conversion rates remain below expectations. Engineering is near capacity, with the analytics dashboard targeted for August. Reporting performance issues are affecting both existing customers (94% retention) and new prospects. Hiring decisions and the annual customer conference budget were both deferred pending more information.

💡 Key Takeaways
Q2 revenue grew 12%, missing the committed 18% target. Gap driven by 5 of 14 expected enterprise contracts slipping to Q3.
Website traffic increased 34% QoQ, but conversion rates remain below expectations. Website redesign and simplified demo-request process are planned.
Customer retention is strong at 94%, but support ticket volume has increased significantly — mostly complaints about reporting performance and dashboard loading times.
Engineering is near capacity with the analytics dashboard targeted for August. Adding website work may require external contractors — cost estimate not yet available.
Reporting performance issues are being raised during sales calls by prospects, making it a cross-functional priority issue beyond just customer success.
Hiring plan decisions and the customer conference budget are both deferred — hiring until Q3 forecasts are finalized; conference until final vendor proposals are received.
