## Day 18 of Claude Challenge:

## What we'd learnt today
Custom Skills allow Claude to remember complex workflows. Instead of manually organizing notes every time, you can create a reusable skill that automatically transforms unstructured information into project dashboards, action plans, and decision logs.

1
Reusable Workflows: Create once and use forever.
2
Information Management: Convert messy notes into structured outputs.
3
Project Planning: Generate action plans, risks, and decisions automatically.
4
Productivity: Reduce manual organization work significantly.

## Key Learnings

* Learned how to create and configure a reusable Custom Skill in Claude using predefined instructions.
* Observed that the skill successfully transformed unstructured notes into structured dashboards without requiring manual analysis.
* Tested the skill on multiple note formats, including a meeting transcript and brainstorming notes, to evaluate consistency.
* Found that the skill automatically extracted summaries, action items, risks, blockers, dependencies, stakeholders, timelines, and open questions.
* Noticed that meeting notes generated decision-focused outputs, while brainstorming notes produced planning-focused outputs, showing the skill's adaptability.
* Learned the importance of identifying priorities, risks, and follow-up actions from large volumes of text.
* Understood how interactive dashboards improve readability and make information easier to review.
* Verified that the Custom Skill can be reused repeatedly without re-entering instructions, increasing productivity and consistency.
* Gained experience in documenting AI-generated outputs and maintaining a GitHub portfolio of completed tasks.
* Explored a practical use case of AI for project management, meeting analysis, and idea organization.
