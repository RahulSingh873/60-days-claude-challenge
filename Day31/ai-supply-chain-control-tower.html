AI Supply Chain Control Tower

A 3-minute operations command simulation
🧑‍💼
You are the Head of Operations.
Alerts will stream in from across your global supply chain. Choose the right action, fast, to protect service levels and revenue.

How it works

  • Alerts appear with a priority (Critical / High / Medium) and a countdown.
  • Pick the best action before the alert timer runs out.
  • Good decisions boost KPIs and Score. Bad ones hurt them.
  • Ignoring or letting an alert expire always hurts performance.
  • Difficulty ramps up — more alerts, faster, as the clock runs down.

📖 Instructions

Objective

Keep the global supply chain running smoothly for 3 minutes by resolving alerts with the best possible action.

KPIs

  • Service Level, CSAT, Inventory Health, Transport Efficiency — higher is better.
  • Operating Cost — lower is better.
  • Revenue Protected — accumulates as you make good calls.

Actions

Each alert offers several response options. Some are ideal for that situation, some are mediocre, and some are poor. "Ignore" and "Delay Decision" carry risk — delays can trigger extra consequences later.

Scoring

Correct, decisive action = high score gain. Wrong or ignored alerts reduce KPIs, raise cost, and cost score.
