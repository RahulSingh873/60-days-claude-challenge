# Day 31 – AI Supply Chain Control Tower

## Objective
Built and tested an AI-powered Supply Chain Control Tower simulation using HTML. The goal was to manage real-time operational alerts, make quick decisions under time pressure, and maintain key business KPIs such as service level, inventory, transport, customer satisfaction, operational cost, and revenue.


## Key Learnings
- Learned how AI can assist in real-time supply chain decision-making.
- Understood the importance of prioritizing critical operational alerts.
- Improved decision-making under time constraints.
- Observed how different actions directly impact business KPIs.
- Gained insight into balancing customer satisfaction, inventory, transportation, and operational costs.
- Experienced how multiple disruptions can be managed simultaneously using a centralized control tower approach.
- Understood the value of fast and accurate decisions in protecting revenue and maintaining operational efficiency.

## Results
- Successfully completed the simulation.
- Achieved an **A+** performance rating.
- Maintained excellent KPI performance throughout the simulation.
- Resolved the majority of operational alerts with high decision accuracy.
