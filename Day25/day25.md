# 🦈 Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Startup Name
**CampusConnect AI**

---

# Objective

Develop an AI-powered Shark Tank Simulator that evaluates startup ideas through investor-style questioning, business analysis, scoring, and investment decisions.

The startup pitched was **CampusConnect AI**, an AI-powered employability platform that helps students build industry-ready skills, assists colleges in improving placement outcomes, and connects recruiters with qualified candidates.

---

# Startup Details

### Problem Statement

Students often graduate without industry-ready skills, career guidance, or internship opportunities.

### Solution

An AI-powered career platform that assesses skills, recommends personalized learning paths, builds resumes, and matches students with internships and jobs.

### Revenue Model

- College Subscriptions
- Recruitment Commissions
- Premium Student Plans

### Target Audience

- College Students
- Universities
- Recruiters

### Funding Ask

**₹20 Lakhs**

### Equity Offered

**10%**

# Key Learnings

- A successful startup needs a clear and defensible competitive advantage.
- Investors prioritize customer traction and market validation over ideas alone.
- A recurring revenue model improves long-term business sustainability.
- A well-defined go-to-market strategy is critical for scaling.
- Strong institutional partnerships and proprietary data create a lasting competitive moat.
- Transparency about business metrics such as traction and revenue builds investor trust.
- Execution and continuous improvement are more important than having a unique idea.
