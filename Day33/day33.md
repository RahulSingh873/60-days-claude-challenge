## 🎯 Key Learnings

- Sensational headlines often exaggerate reality to attract attention, even when the underlying story is routine.
- Emotional manipulation commonly relies on urgency, fear, exclusivity, and FOMO to influence quick decisions.
- Evaluating the credibility of a source is just as important as analyzing the headline itself.
- Understanding the intended audience helps reveal why specific language and messaging are used.
- Reading beyond the headline and verifying facts before reacting is one of the most effective media literacy habits.
- Neutral, evidence-based wording communicates information more accurately than emotionally charged language.
