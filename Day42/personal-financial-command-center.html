    Tips, Checklist & Resources

    Bite-sized financial literacy, plus a checklist to build momentum this month.

    TIP 01
    Track every expense for one week before setting a budget — you can't fix what you haven't measured.
    TIP 02
    Automate a fixed sum to savings the day allowance arrives, so it never feels like "leftover" money.
    TIP 03
    Split spending using the 50/30/20 rule as a rough guide: needs, wants, savings.
    TIP 04
    Compare prices before buying textbooks — used copies, library, or PDFs can cut this cost sharply.
    TIP 05
    Review your expense log weekly, not just at month-end, to catch small leaks early.
    TIP 06
    Keep a small emergency buffer separate from goal savings — even ₹1,000 helps.
