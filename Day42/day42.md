# Day 42 – Personal Financial Command Center

## 🎯 Objective

Build an AI-powered Personal Financial Command Center that helps students manage income, expenses, budgets, savings goals, financial health, and future financial planning through interactive simulations and AI-driven insights.

---
## 💡 Key Learnings

- Learned how AI can simplify personal finance management through interactive dashboards.
- Explored how income, expenses, budgets, and savings work together to determine financial health.
- Understood the importance of tracking spending habits and maintaining a positive saving rate.
- Used what-if simulations to evaluate different financial decisions before making them.
- Gained experience building a complete browser-based financial management application using Claude.
