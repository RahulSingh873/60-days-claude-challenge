# Day 28 -- 🏥 Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator

## Overview

This project is an interactive **Hospital Admission Readiness Simulator** designed to demonstrate the hospital admission workflow. Users can configure different admission scenarios, evaluate admission readiness, complete workflow actions, and understand how documentation, insurance verification, physician orders, and prior authorization affect patient admission.

## Key Learnings

Building this simulator helped me understand several important healthcare operations concepts:

* Prior Authorization is one of the most critical checkpoints before hospital admission.
* Admission readiness depends on multiple interconnected workflows rather than a single approval.
* Clinical documentation directly impacts medical necessity validation and payer decisions.
* Insurance verification, physician orders, patient consent, and bed availability all influence admission readiness.
* Care coordination between physicians, nurses, utilization review teams, and case managers is essential for efficient hospital operations.
* Risk tracking helps identify bottlenecks early and supports informed admission decisions.
* Interactive simulations provide a practical way to understand complex healthcare workflows and improve operational thinking.
