## Key Learnings

### Customer Acquisition Matters More Than Features

A strong product alone is not enough. Distribution and acquisition channels determine growth.

### Sustainable Revenue Requires Multiple Streams

Combining training programs, consulting, and partnerships creates a more resilient business model.

### First 100 Users Are Critical

Early adopters provide validation, feedback, and testimonials that drive future growth.

### Competitive Advantage Comes From Execution

Local partnerships, trust, and community engagement can become long-term moats.

### Data-Driven Decision Making

Tracking acquisition cost, conversion rates, and engagement metrics is essential for scalability.
