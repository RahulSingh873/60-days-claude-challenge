# Recommended Primary Model

## Claude Sonnet

### Why This Model Fits Me

Claude Sonnet provides the best balance between:

* Speed
* Accuracy
* Reasoning
* Cost Efficiency
* Long-form Content Generation

For a student working on learning, projects, resumes, LinkedIn posts, and research, Sonnet delivers excellent performance without requiring the higher cost and processing time of Opus.

---

# When to Use Haiku

Use Haiku for:

* Quick questions
* Definitions
* Summaries
* Simple explanations
* Brainstorming ideas
* Fast productivity tasks

### Examples

* Explain a concept in one paragraph
* Summarize a PDF
* Generate quick notes
* Create a short email

---

# When to Use Sonnet

Use Sonnet for:

* Resume optimization

* Research projects

* Structured reports

# When to Use Opus

Use Opus only when:

* Solving highly complex problems
* Deep strategic planning
* Multi-step reasoning
* Advanced research synthesis
* Large project planning

### Examples

* Startup business strategy
* Multi-phase village development plan
* Extensive research reports
* Long-term career roadmap

# Biggest Mistakes I Should Avoid

1. Using Opus for simple tasks.
2. Starting with High or Max effort unnecessarily.
3. Giving vague prompts without context.
4. Not assigning a role when asking complex questions.
5. Accepting the first output without refinement.
6. Forgetting to provide examples of desired output.
7. Mixing multiple objectives into one prompt.
