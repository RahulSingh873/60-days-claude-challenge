# Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: AI Supply Chain Crisis Simulator

## Objective

Build and explore an AI-powered supply chain crisis management simulator using Claude. The objective was to understand how executive decisions, supplier negotiations, leadership communication, and AI investments influence business resilience, operational performance, and overall supply chain outcomes during unexpected disruptions.

## Key Learnings

* Supply chain disruptions require quick, data-driven decision-making to minimize operational and financial impact.
* Every crisis response involves trade-offs between cost, delivery timelines, customer satisfaction, and long-term business stability.
* Effective supplier negotiation is essential for maintaining continuity during periods of uncertainty.
* Leadership communication plays a significant role in building stakeholder confidence during a crisis.
* Strategic AI investments can improve forecasting accuracy, risk detection, inventory planning, and supply chain resilience.
* Executive dashboards help visualize key performance metrics and measure the impact of strategic decisions.
* Running multiple simulation scenarios demonstrates how different leadership approaches produce different business outcomes.
* AI-powered simulations provide a practical environment for developing real-world decision-making and crisis management skills.
