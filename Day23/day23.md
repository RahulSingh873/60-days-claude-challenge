# Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Project
DigiLocal – Local Business Digitization Service

## Problem Statement
Many MSMEs in India lack:
- Google Business Profile
- Website
- Online ordering capability

This makes them digitally invisible to potential customers.

---

## Target Customers

### Primary Segments
1. Restaurants & Dhabas
2. Kirana Stores
3. Salons & Beauty Parlours
4. Coaching Institutes

### Customer Characteristics
- Low digital literacy
- Uses WhatsApp daily
- Physical storefront
- Revenue above ₹30,000/month

---

## Key Customer Insights

### Insight 1
Business owners want more customers but don't know how to create an online presence.

### Insight 2
Trust is a major barrier due to previous bad experiences with freelancers.

### Insight 3
Google Maps visibility is perceived as the fastest way to gain customers.

### Insight 4
WhatsApp is the preferred communication channel.

---

## MVP Recommendation

### Must Have Features
- Google Business Profile Setup
- WhatsApp Business Setup
- Mobile-Friendly Website
- Online Ordering Link

### Avoid Building Initially
- Mobile App
- AI Chatbot
- Custom Website Builder
- POS Integration

---

## Customer Journey

1. Awareness
2. Consideration

## Key Learnings

1. MSMEs need simple solutions rather than complex software.
2. Google Business Profile provides the quickest visible value.
3. Trust-building is more important than advanced features.
4. WhatsApp should be the primary engagement channel.
5. Start with one city before expanding.
