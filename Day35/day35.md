# Key Learnings

- Learned that an effective prompt should clearly define the **task**, **role**, **context**, **constraints**, and **expected output**.
- Understood the importance of removing irrelevant or distracting instructions to improve AI response quality.
- Practiced designing prompts that balance clarity, specificity, and completeness without unnecessary complexity.
- Discovered that providing sufficient context enables AI to generate more accurate and relevant responses.
- Learned how constraints help preserve intended behavior and reduce ambiguous outputs.
- Recognized that specifying the desired output format leads to more structured and consistent responses.
- Compared weak, optimized, and over-engineered prompts to understand why concise, well-scoped prompts perform better.
- Gained hands-on experience with prompt engineering principles through interactive scenarios involving prompt construction, refinement, and evaluation.
- Improved my understanding of how prompt quality directly influences the usefulness, accuracy, and reliability of AI-generated outputs.
