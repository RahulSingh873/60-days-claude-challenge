## Analysis Findings

Diesel remains the most practical fuel choice for the Scorpio-N

Running cost is approximately ₹4.67/km, significantly lower than Petrol (₹6.15/km) and E85 (₹6.37/km).

For mixed city-highway usage, diesel offers the best balance of power, mileage, and affordability.

Monthly fuel expenditure is manageable

At 1,500 km per month, estimated fuel spending is around ₹7,011/month.

This makes diesel a cost-effective option for moderate to high monthly usage.

E85 presents an interesting sustainability paradox

Despite having a lower pump price than petrol, E85 becomes more expensive per kilometer because of its lower energy density.

The dashboard identifies a break-even fuel price of ₹79.11/L, below which E85 would become financially competitive.

EVs offer the lowest operating cost

EV running cost is only ₹1.75/km, making it the most economical fuel type.

However, charging time and infrastructure limitations reduce its practicality for long-distance SUV travel.

Environmental impact varies significantly

E85 has the lowest CO₂ emissions among liquid fuels.

Diesel produces the highest emissions among conventional fuel options, highlighting the trade-off between cost efficiency and sustainability.

Vehicle age increases maintenance costs

Maintenance expenses rise steadily as vehicles age.

For diesel vehicles, maintenance cost can nearly double in older age brackets, emphasizing the importance of preventive maintenance.

CNG provides strong economic benefits

CNG delivers a lower running cost than diesel.

However, reduced boot space and limited refueling infrastructure may affect convenience for SUV owners.

## Key Learnings from Today's Task

Fuel price alone does not determine affordability.

Real-world efficiency and energy density have a major impact on cost per kilometer.

Data visualization improves decision-making.

KPI cards, charts, gauges, and comparison tables make complex vehicle cost data easier to understand.

A holistic vehicle analysis should include multiple factors.

Fuel cost, maintenance, emissions, vehicle age, and convenience all influence ownership costs.

Sustainable options are not always the cheapest.

E85 reduces emissions but currently does not outperform diesel in operating cost.

EVs demonstrate the future potential of low-cost mobility.

Their running costs are significantly lower, though infrastructure remains a challenge.

Vehicle age is a critical cost driver.

Maintenance expenses increase over time and should be considered when evaluating long-term ownership.

Interactive dashboards transform raw CSV data into actionable insights.

AI-generated dashboards can quickly convert large datasets into business-ready visual reports.

AI can accelerate data analysis workflows.

Using Claude to process a CSV dataset and generate a complete HTML dashboard significantly reduced development time while maintaining analytical depth.
