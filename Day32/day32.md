# Day 32 – Marketing Strategy Simulator

## Objective

Build and use an AI-powered Marketing Strategy Simulator in Claude that creates customized marketing strategies for businesses or personal brands, generates a 30-day marketing roadmap, adapts to dynamic marketing events, and produces a final growth report.

---

## Key Learnings

- Learned how AI can generate complete marketing strategies from minimal business information.
- Understood the importance of identifying the target audience before planning campaigns.
- Explored how different marketing platforms suit different audience segments and business goals.
- Learned to organize marketing efforts around focused content pillars for consistent messaging.
- Experienced generating a structured 30-day marketing roadmap using AI.
- Practiced adapting marketing strategies in response to randomized real-world marketing events.
- Understood how AI can summarize campaign performance through a Growth Report.
- Learned reusable Claude prompting techniques that can be applied to future marketing and business strategy projects.
