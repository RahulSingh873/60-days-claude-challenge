## 🎯 Key Learnings

- High marketing performance metrics do not always indicate customer satisfaction or campaign success.
- Transparent messaging is essential—omitting important conditions can damage customer trust.
- Marketing claims must accurately reflect the actual product experience to avoid misleading customers.
- Operational issues, such as delayed referral rewards, can negatively impact the perception of an otherwise successful campaign.
- Combining customer feedback with campaign data is the most effective way to identify the true root cause of a marketing failure.
- Effective marketing requires close alignment between messaging, product capabilities, and operational execution.
