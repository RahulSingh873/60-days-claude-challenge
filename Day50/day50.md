# Day 50 – Defend Your Experience

## 🚀 Project Overview

Built an AI-powered **Defend Your Experience** interview simulator that evaluates resume claims through realistic HR, technical, and managerial cross-examination. The application analyzes uploaded resumes or portfolios, challenges each claim with interview-style questions, and generates a Defense Report highlighting strengths, weak areas, and overall interview readiness.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Build an AI-powered interview preparation platform.
- Practice defending resume and project claims.
- Simulate real HR, Technical, and Manager interview scenarios.
- Evaluate confidence and depth of technical knowledge.
- Generate personalized interview readiness feedback.

---

## 🛠️ Features

- Resume and portfolio upload support
- Multiple interview modes
  - Panel (HR + Tech + Manager)
  - Friendly HR
  - Tough Technical
  - Rapid-fire
- Internship and company-specific preparation
- AI-generated cross-examination of every resume claim
- Defense readiness score
- Claim-wise improvement suggestions
- Modern responsive UI with dark theme

---

## 📚 Key Learnings

- Every resume bullet should be backed by real implementation experience.
- Strong interview answers require explaining the complete development process, not just outcomes.
- Specific examples and technical details improve credibility during interviews.
- AI can effectively simulate realistic interview pressure for better preparation.
- Identifying weak resume claims early helps improve confidence before actual interviews.
