### Day 16 – Custom Skill Creation & Stock Fundamental Research
## Objective

The objective of Day 16 was to learn how to create and utilize a Custom Skill in Claude for conducting detailed stock fundamental analysis. The task involved creating a reusable skill, generating stock research reports, analyzing valuation metrics, ownership patterns, financial health, growth prospects, and identifying key risk factors.

## Custom Skill Details

Skill Name: stock-fundamental-research

Purpose

This custom skill was designed to automate stock fundamental analysis and generate structured research reports covering:

Company Overview
Financial Performance
Valuation Analysis
Growth Metrics
Profitability Ratios
Shareholding Patterns
Risk Assessment
Investment Outlook
Stock Analyzed

Company: Tata Consultancy Services (TCS)

Sector: Information Technology Services

Exchange: NSE: TCS

### Key Learnings
Learned how to create and configure reusable Custom Skills in Claude.
Understood how AI can automate stock fundamental research and generate structured reports.
Learned to evaluate valuation metrics such as P/E Ratio, P/B Ratio, Dividend Yield, and EV/EBITDA.
Gained practical knowledge of financial ratios including ROE, ROCE, Operating Margin, and Free Cash Flow.
Learned how shareholding patterns and promoter holdings influence investment decisions.
Understood the importance of identifying business risks alongside growth opportunities.
Learned how AI-generated research can improve productivity and reduce manual analysis time.
Observed how Custom Skills can be reused across multiple stocks without repeatedly entering complex prompts.
Improved understanding of the Indian IT sector through detailed analysis of TCS.
Learned the importance of combining quantitative metrics with qualitative analysis before making investment decisions.
