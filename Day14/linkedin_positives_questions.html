5 Smart Interview Questions
Ask these to expose what the job description is hiding
Junior Full Stack Developer
1
Seniority & Compensation
"This role is listed as Junior but requires 5+ years of experience and includes responsibilities like architecture design and mentoring. Can you clarify the actual seniority level — and how will compensation reflect those expectations?"
Why it matters: Forces them to either admit the role is mislabelled or commit to senior-level pay. Their answer tells you everything about how they value talent.
Validates
Pay Fairness
2
Remote Authenticity
"The listing says remote, but mentions that relocation to Bengaluru may be required after probation. What specific conditions trigger that requirement — and is it truly optional or is it a formality?"
Why it matters: Catches the bait-and-switch early. If they hesitate, the relocation is non-negotiable and the "remote" label is misleading.
Validates
Location Truth
3
Burnout & On-Call Risk
"The JD mentions 24/7 support responsibilities. How is on-call structured — is there a rotation, additional compensation for after-hours work, and what does an average week actually look like for someone in this role?"
Why it matters: Exposes whether "24/7 support" means a fair rotation or everyone is always on the hook. No structured answer = red flag confirmed.
Validates
Work-Life Reality
4
Financial Stability
"The company is 8 months old and I couldn't find public funding information. Can you share the current funding stage, approximate runway, and how you're planning to sustain operations and payroll over the next 12–18 months?"
Why it matters: A legitimate company will answer this confidently. Vagueness or deflection signals cash flow problems — your salary may not be safe.
Validates
Financial Safety
5
Culture & Retention
"Employee reviews mention long working hours. What's the actual average weekly workload for this role — and what concrete policies does the company have to protect personal time and prevent burnout?"
Why it matters: "We work hard but we play hard" is not a policy. Push for specifics. No concrete answer = the culture is exactly what the reviews describe.
Validates
Culture Reality
