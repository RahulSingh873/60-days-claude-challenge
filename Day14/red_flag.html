🚩 Top Red Flags
10
Experience paradox — "Junior" with 5+ years required
0–2 yrs = junior. 5+ yrs = mid/senior. Classic bait-and-switch to underpay senior talent.
10
Unrealistic stack — 12 technologies for one person
React + Angular + Vue + Node + Python + Java + Go + AWS + Azure + GCP + Docker + K8s. That's a team, not a hire.
9
Fake remote — relocation to Bengaluru after probation
"Remote" that flips to mandatory relocation is not remote. Deliberate misrepresentation.
9
24/7 on-call support for a junior dev
Severe burnout signal. Likely violates labour standards. No mention of rotation or compensation.
8
No salary + 8-month-old startup, no funding info
Opacity on money = power imbalance. Zero salary range is a deliberate negotiation trap.
8
Toxic culture dog-whistles throughout
"Work hard play hard" · "Fast-paced" · "Thrive under pressure" · "We're family" = overwork with no overtime.
7
Junior asked to lead clients, mentor & architect
Senior responsibilities, junior pay. The company wants a team lead for an intern salary.
6
Long hours in reviews + rapid hiring surge
High churn signal. People are leaving fast and the startup is scrambling to backfill quietly.

💡 5 Smart Interview Questions to Ask Anyway
1
This is listed as "Junior" but requires 5+ yrs and senior responsibilities. What does compensation actually look like?
→ Experience paradox + pay fairness
2
The role says remote but mentions relocation. What are the exact conditions — is relocation truly optional?
→ Remote authenticity + hidden requirement
3
How is 24/7 support structured — rotation, overtime pay, and what does a typical week look like?
→ Burnout risk + work-life balance
4
What's the current funding status and runway? How do you plan to sustain payroll over the next 18 months?
→ Financial stability + company risk
5
Reviews mention long hours. What's the average weekly workload and how does the company protect personal time?
→ Culture signals + actual hours
