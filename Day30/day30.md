## What I Built
A React-based Supply Chain Builder simulator that allows users to:
- Generate a random company profile.
- Make five major supply chain decisions.
- Track real-time changes in business metrics.
- View a final optimization dashboard with performance analysis.
- Replay the simulation with different companies.

---

## Key Features
- Random company generation
- Interactive decision-making workflow
- Live metric updates after every decision
- Overall Supply Chain Score
- Strengths & Weaknesses analysis
- Biggest Risk identification
- Practical improvement recommendations
- Decision recap dashboard

---

## My Results

**Overall Supply Chain Score:** **58/100**

### Final Metrics

| Metric | Score |
|---------|------:|
| Cost Efficiency | 34 |
| Delivery Speed | 54 |
| Resilience | 78 |
| Customer Satisfaction | 74 |
| Sustainability | 50 |

### Strengths
- Resilience
- Customer Satisfaction

### Weakness
- Cost Efficiency

### Biggest Risk
Low cost efficiency could negatively impact long-term operational performance.

### Suggested Improvements
- Move fulfillment closer to customers.
- Reduce expensive transportation and warehousing costs.
- Shift transportation towards more sustainable options.

---

## Decisions Made
- Supplier Strategy: Multiple Suppliers
- Factory Location: Nearshore (Regional)
- Warehouse Strategy: Micro-Fulfillment Network
- Transportation Method: Sea Freight
- Inventory Strategy: Balanced Inventory

---

## Key Learnings
- Every supply chain decision requires balancing cost, speed, resilience, customer satisfaction, and sustainability.
- Using multiple suppliers improves resilience but may increase operational costs.
- Nearshore manufacturing provides a good balance between delivery speed and supply chain reliability.
- Warehouse and transportation choices directly influence operational efficiency and customer experience.
- The best supply chain strategy is one that balances multiple business objectives instead of optimizing only one.
