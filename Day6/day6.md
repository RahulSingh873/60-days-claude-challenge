## MY KEY LEARNINGS

1. Learned how Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) scan and filter resumes before they reach recruiters.
2. Understood the importance of using relevant keywords from job descriptions to improve ATS compatibility.
3. Learned how proper resume formatting improves readability for both ATS software and human recruiters.
4. Discovered how AI tools like Claude can identify weaknesses and suggest improvements in resumes.
5. Learned to quantify achievements using numbers and measurable results rather than generic statements.
6. Understood the importance of keeping a resume concise and focused, preferably within one page.
7. Learned how to tailor a resume according to specific job roles and industry requirements.
8. Gained hands-on experience using AI to analyze, optimize, and enhance professional documents.
