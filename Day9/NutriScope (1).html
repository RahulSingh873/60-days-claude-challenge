Nutrition Sources & References
Scientific and institutional references powering NutriScope
🏛️ Dietary Guidelines & RDAs
1
ICMR-NIN Recommended Dietary Allowances (2020)
Indian Council of Medical Research — National Institute of Nutrition. RDA and EAR values for Indian population across age and gender groups.
2
WHO Nutrient Reference Values
World Health Organization guidelines for macronutrients, micronutrients and energy intake.
3
NIH Office of Dietary Supplements
Fact sheets for vitamins and minerals including Vitamin D, B12, Iron, Calcium, Folate, Potassium, Magnesium, and Zinc.
4
Dietary Reference Intakes (DRI) — IOM/National Academies
DRI values for macronutrients, energy, vitamins, and minerals. Used for protein, fat, carbohydrate, and fiber targets.
🔬 Food Composition Data
5
IFCT 2017 — Indian Food Composition Tables
Published by NIN, Hyderabad. Primary source for per-100g nutritional values of Indian foods in this database.
6
USDA FoodData Central (2024)
Used for international foods and cross-validation of macronutrient values. Covers 100 foods in this database.
7
Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (1990)
Mifflin MD et al. "A new predictive equation for resting energy expenditure." Used for BMR calculation. Am J Clin Nutr. 51(2):241-7.
8
FAO/WHO/UNU Expert Consultation on Energy
Physical Activity Level (PAL) multipliers for TDEE calculation. Human Energy Requirements Report, 2004.
💊 Micronutrient Science
9
Vitamin D Deficiency — Endocrine Society (2011)
Holick MF et al. Clinical practice guideline on Vitamin D deficiency. J Clin Endocrinol Metab. 96(7):1911-30.
10
Iron Deficiency Anaemia — WHO (2001)
Iron Deficiency Anaemia: Assessment, Prevention, and Control. WHO/NHD/01.3. Used for iron risk thresholds.
11
Vitamin B12 Deficiency in India — Yajnik et al.
Yajnik CS et al. "Vitamin B12 deficiency and hyperhomocysteinaemia in rural and urban Indians." J Assoc Physicians India, 2006.
